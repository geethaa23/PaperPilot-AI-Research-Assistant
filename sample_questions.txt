PaperPilot – sample questions

1. What is the main objective of this paper?
2. Explain the proposed methodology step by step.
3. Which algorithms or machine learning models are used?
4. What dataset is used? Mention its size and source if available.
5. What evaluation metrics are reported?
6. What accuracy, precision, recall or F1-score is achieved?
7. What are the major findings and limitations?
8. What future work do the authors suggest?
9. Compare the methodologies used in Paper A and Paper B.
10. Which paper reports better performance, and are the metrics comparable?
